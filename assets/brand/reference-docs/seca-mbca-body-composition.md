## What it is

A medical-grade body composition analyzer (the seca mBCA, available in Alpha/Pro/Ultra models) that measures fat mass, skeletal muscle mass, water content, visceral fat and bone mass in seconds — going beyond what a standard scale shows.

## Key points from the seca patient one-pager

- Body composition analysis gives a comprehensive health/fitness assessment beyond weight alone: muscle mass, body fat %, water content, bone mass.
- Helps build personalized training/nutrition plans and monitor progress over time.
- Measures: Skeletal Muscle Mass (SMM), Fat Mass, Fat Mass Index (FMI), Visceral Adipose Tissue (VAT), TRU Body Score (a composite score, 100 = average for gender/age/height).
- Visceral fat around abdominal organs is called out specifically as a diabetes/cardiovascular risk factor independent of overall weight.
- Measurement takes only a few seconds; results are immediate.
- Precision verified in clinical studies; uses German measuring technology (seca is the world market leader in medical measuring/weighing).
- Results can sync to the **seca myAnalytics app** for anytime tracking.
