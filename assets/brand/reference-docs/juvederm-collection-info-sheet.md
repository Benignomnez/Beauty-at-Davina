# Juvéderm Collection Info Sheet

Source: `Juvederm Collection Info Sheet.docx`, provided by the PM via the shared Google Drive folder (added 2026-07-29). Extracted text preserved verbatim below for reference when writing site copy.

## 1. JUVÉDERM Ultra XC

**Description**
- Smooth hyaluronic acid gel
- Provides noticeable volume while remaining soft
- Excellent hydration because it attracts water

**FDA-approved Uses**
- Lip augmentation
- Perioral (around-the-mouth) correction
- Moderate nasolabial folds

**Common Off-label Uses**
- Oral commissures
- Cupid's bow
- Fine smile lines

**Ideal Patient**
- Wants fuller lips
- First-time filler patient
- Younger patients

**Duration**: Lips: Up to 1 year

**Characteristics**: Soft · Hydrating · Creates noticeable fullness

## 2. JUVÉDERM Ultra Plus XC

**Description**
- Similar to Ultra XC but denser
- Designed for deeper wrinkles requiring more support

**FDA-approved Uses**
- Moderate to severe nasolabial folds

**Common Off-label Uses**
- Marionette lines
- Pre-jowl sulcus
- Chin
- Mild cheek augmentation

**Ideal Patient**
- Older patient
- Deep facial folds
- Needs structural support

**Duration**: 12–18 months

**Characteristics**: More lifting than Ultra · Better for deeper folds · Higher G' (stiffer gel)

## 3. JUVÉDERM VOLBELLA XC

Uses Allergan's Vycross Technology, making it one of the softest fillers.

**FDA-approved Uses**
- Lips
- Perioral lines
- Undereye hollows (tear troughs)

**Common Off-label Uses**
- Lip hydration
- Fine smile lines
- Earlobes
- Superficial corrections

**Ideal Patient**
- Wants subtle enhancement
- Thin lips
- Natural-looking lips
- Tear trough correction

**Duration**: Lips: Up to 12 months. Undereyes: Around 12 months in many patients

**Characteristics**: Lowest swelling · Softest filler · Natural movement · Excellent for delicate areas

## 4. JUVÉDERM VOLLURE XC

Also made with Vycross Technology.

**FDA-approved Uses**
- Moderate to severe nasolabial folds

**Common Off-label Uses**
- Marionette lines
- Oral commissures
- Chin
- Jawline blending
- Mild cheeks

**Ideal Patient**
- Wants natural movement
- Moderate facial folds
- Younger or middle-aged patient

**Duration**: Up to 18 months

**Characteristics**: Flexible · Natural expression · Moderate lift · Low water attraction

## 5. JUVÉDERM VOLUMA XC

The highest lifting capacity before VOLUX.

**FDA-approved Uses**
- Cheeks
- Chin
- Temples

**Common Off-label Uses**
- Midface
- Lateral cheek
- Zygomatic arch
- Facial balancing

**Ideal Patient**
- Volume loss
- Facial contouring
- Non-surgical facelift

**Duration**: Cheeks: Up to 24 months. Chin: Up to 12 months. Temples: Up to 13 months

**Characteristics**: Excellent lift · Strong projection · Deep structural support

## 6. JUVÉDERM VOLUX XC

Newest and firmest filler in the collection.

**FDA-approved Uses**
- Jawline

**Common Off-label Uses**
- Chin
- Mandibular angle
- Facial masculinization
- Facial balancing

**Ideal Patient**
- Wants a defined jawline
- Weak jawline
- Jowls
- Lower face contouring

**Duration**: Up to 12 months

**Characteristics**: Highest G' · Best structural support · Sharp definition · Minimal spread
