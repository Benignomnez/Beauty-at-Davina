About Beauty at Davina

Beauty that begins with well-being.

Our story

Beauty at Davina didn’t start as a new business. It started inside Davina Medical Care, as a response to what we saw in the patients we cared for every day.

Time and again, we recognized a gap, patients who wanted more than treatment for what was wrong. They deserved a partner in staying well, aging well, and feeling like themselves for years to come. So we built Beauty at Davina to meet that need, a wellness and aesthetics division rooted in the same medicine, expertise, and physician oversight they already trusted.

Today we’re proud to care for our community across Miami-Dade — one honest conversation and one natural-looking result at a time.

Beauty at Davina is a service of Davina Medical Care, P.A.
