FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your initial consultation is complimentary and carries no obligation — it’s simply how we get to know your goals and design the right plan.

Do you offer financing?

We offer flexible payment options and financing on many treatments. We’ll walk through the details during your visit.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on the treatment — Botox shows in 3–7 days, fillers are immediate, and skincare and wellness programs build over time. We’ll set clear expectations upfront.

Are treatments performed by medical professionals?

Always. Every injectable and medical treatment is performed or supervised by our licensed medical team.
